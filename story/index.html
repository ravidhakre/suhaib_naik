01 — Who is Suheb Naik?

A construction professional turned real-estate developer.

Suheb Naik is a Goa-based construction professional, real-estate entrepreneur and developer with over nine years of experience across the built environment.

His journey began in 2017 with interiors through Workzplus, before moving into contracting and project execution through ASN Associates. Over the years, he has been involved in more than 62 projects across residential, commercial, industrial, restoration, infrastructure and land development.

That experience has given him a practical understanding of property beyond buying and selling — from land and feasibility to design, construction, market requirements and development.

Today, through Memaar, Suheb is focused on real-estate development and identifying opportunities across selected markets in Goa, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Kerala.

02 — The journey

From leadership to land.
From construction to development.

The route into real estate was not a straight line. It began with leadership, business experiments, interiors and site execution — and only then moved into development.

Chapter 01 2013–15

The student leader

At Government Polytechnic, Panjim, Suheb was elected General Secretary in 2015. He organised a national-level technical paper presentation and expo, brought industry to the campus, and invited the Chief Minister to visit the college for the first time. The early lesson was simple: a vision becomes real through organisation, people and follow-through.

Chapter 02 2013–16

Learning business the hard way

Before construction, there were experiments: mobile and scrap dealing, goat farming, fish breeding, Meal Hunt, a biryani venture, Eagle Agro, Ganga Agro, coconut distribution and a campus canteen. Some worked for a while. Some did not. The lesson was never failure — it was what the market, the customer and the operation taught him next.

Chapter 03 2017

Interiors, then site

The professional journey began with interiors through Workzplus. Working closely with homes and spaces built an early understanding of how design affects the way people live. From there, the work moved into contracting and project execution through ASN Associates — from drawings on a table to decisions on a live site.

Chapter 04 2019–25

62+ projects, one foundation

Across residential, commercial, industrial, restoration, infrastructure and land development, the work added up to more than 62 projects. Villas, apartments, homes, roads, industrial sheds, old-house restorations and plotted layouts. Different scales, different challenges, the same foundation: practical experience.

Chapter 05 Today

From executing to developing

Through Memaar, the next chapter is development: identifying land, testing feasibility, shaping concepts and bringing projects to life across Goa, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Kerala. The question has not changed. It has simply moved earlier in the process: what can this land become?

04 — How I work

Four principles that do not change.

They come from the site, not from a brand deck. They are the checks I run before a property decision is allowed to move forward.

01

Build first

Understand what it takes to actually make the thing. The drawing, the cost, the site and the finish are connected. A decision that ignores any of them is incomplete.

02

Ask better questions

The first question is rarely the most useful one. Before the price, ask about access, potential, risk and what the property could become.

03

Stay accountable

A project does not end at the handover or the transaction. The details after the decision are where a reputation is actually built.

04

Keep learning

Every project, every market and every conversation adds something. The work is to stay curious enough to use it.

05 — Beyond the site

Leadership did not stop at the campus.

Alongside the business, Suheb serves as State President of Rifah Goa, working with a community organisation on education, welfare and entrepreneurship initiatives across the state. He also mentors young people who are trying to find their own route into business.

The thread is consistent: create the conditions for other people to build something. That is as true of a project team as it is of a student with an idea.
